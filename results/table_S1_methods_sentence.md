Bad channels were identified in 26 of 42 participants and interpolated with spherical splines (1.3 ± 2.6 channels per participant, range 0–16). Gel bridges were detected in 34 participants (21.9 ± 30.8 electrode pairs, range 0–147) and the bridged electrodes were interpolated using virtual channels. ICA removed 2.4 ± 0.7 ocular components per participant (range 2–4). Epochs exceeding 150 µV peak-to-peak were rejected (4.9 ± 7.9% of correct-trial epochs, range 0–42.7%), leaving 472.9 ± 43.8 standard and 74.3 ± 9.1 target epochs per participant (n = 42) and 71.2 ± 9.4 distractor epochs (n = 39).
